# Hackathon 2026 — Idea Submission Template

## Slide 1 — Team Details
- Team Name
- Sr. No.
- Role
- Name
- Academic Year
- Team Leader
- Member 1
- Member 2
- Member 3
- College Name
- Team Leader Contact Number
- Team Leader Email Address

## Slide 2 — Problem Statement Addressed
- Selected problem statement your idea addresses
- Description / Details
- Provide specific details about the problem statement, its context, and significance.

## Slide 3 — Idea Description
- Describe your Idea / Solution / Prototype
- Key Concept & Approach
- Solution Overview

## Slide 4 — Proposed Solution
- Describe your idea in detail
- Solution Details
- Methodology
- Technologies involved
- Implementation strategy

## Slide 5 — Innovation and Uniqueness
- Key Innovation
- Competitive Advantage
- Explain what makes the idea unique/innovative and how it improves on existing alternatives.

## Slide 6 — Impact and Benefits
- Primary Impact
- Quantifiable Outcomes
- Examples: cost reduction, efficiency improvement, etc.

## Slide 7 — Technology & Feasibility / Methodology Used
- Implementation Strategy
- Software Architecture
- Hardware Components
- Development Tools

## Slide 8 — GitHub & Video Link
- GitHub Repository
- Prototype / Simulation Video

## Slide 9 — Research and References
- Research Background & Methodology
- References & Citations
- Ref 1
- Ref 2
- Ref 3

## Submission Instructions
- Maximum 6–7 slides including the title slide.
- Use points, diagrams, infographics, or pictures rather than paragraphs.
- Retain the template’s core content pointers.
- Submit the final solution as PDF.
